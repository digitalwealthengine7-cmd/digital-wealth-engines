What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a business model where you promote products or services from companies and earn a commission when people make purchases through your unique affiliate link.

How It Works

1. Choose a product or service to promote.
2. Get your unique affiliate link.
3. Share valuable content and attract visitors.
4. Earn commissions from successful referrals.
